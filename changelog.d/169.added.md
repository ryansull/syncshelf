- Links follow Hardcover (#169). A fetch that shows a book Hardcover merged into another moves
  its links and every profile's rows on it to that book (`merge` in `change_log`, logged as
  `merges_followed`). A linked book Hardcover no longer returns makes the link `stale` and
  queues its rows `stale`. When a profile's own shelves put a Goodreads book on a different
  Hardcover book from the shared link, that profile's row is queued `profile_conflict` and the
  link stays.
