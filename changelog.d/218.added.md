- Phase 4b credentials: a profile's `goodreads.email` and `goodreads.password` configure the
  Goodreads sign-in (#218). They come as a pair, and need the browser sidecar at
  `services.browser_ws`, so startup refuses credentials without it. With no `browser_ws` the
  session export is off stack-wide, logged once at startup, and Goodreads syncs through RSS and
  `/ingest` only. A profile with no password has session state `disabled`; gaining credentials
  moves it to `needs_sign_in` at the next start, and losing them moves it back.
