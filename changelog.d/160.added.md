- The Goodreads sync engine's first path (#160). A profile's first complete RSS pass is its
  map-only backfill: it links each Goodreads book that Hardcover's Goodreads mapping places on
  the profile's own shelves, queues the rest, and pushes nothing. Later passes push rating
  changes to Hardcover. Runs are recorded in `runs`. Nothing schedules them yet.

  `runs_aborted`.
