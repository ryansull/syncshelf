- MCP tool `reading_stats(period, group_by?, profile?)` (#85): books, pages and hours read in
  a year, month, date range, the last 30 days or 12 months, or all time, optionally grouped by
  month, format, genre, author or rating, with Hardcover goal progress. It says how many
  reads it can't place in any period (undated, or shelved as read with no read logged).
- New config key `profiles.<name>.timezone` (an IANA zone name, default the container's): the
  zone relative dates such as "last 30 days" are read in (#85). New dependency `tzdata`, so
  zone names resolve on any system.
