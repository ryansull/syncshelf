- MCP tool `get_book(book, profile?)` (#86): one book's full record, live from Hardcover:
  details, contributors and narrators, series, top tags, content warnings, ten similar books
  with shelf status, and the caller's shelf (reads, lists, and their own review).
  `household` adds each sharing profile's shelf, never their review. What it reads is
  written back to the copy.
