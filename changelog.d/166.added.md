- The Goodreads RSS sync now runs on its own (#166): each profile with a `goodreads` section
  is read every `cadence.rss_interval` (default `60m`), plus up to a minute of jitter, the
  profiles half an interval apart. A restart resumes the stored schedule, and a loop overdue
  after downtime runs once. A failed or incomplete Goodreads fetch backs off 1 h, 2 h, 4 h,
  then 6 h, resetting on success. Runs older than 90 days are purged daily, with the expired
  OAuth rows (logged as `runs_purged`).
