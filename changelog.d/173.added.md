- MCP tool `unmatched_books` (#173): the caller's Goodreads books that SyncShelf couldn't
  place on a Hardcover book by itself. Each entry says why it's queued, how Goodreads gave
  the book, what resolving it would push ("would mark Read, finished 2026-03-02, rate 4"),
  and the candidates found, with Hardcover links and scores. `refresh` matches the entries
  again first, and `include_ignored` lists ignored ones too.
