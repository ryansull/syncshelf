- Each Goodreads sync first applies the profile's uploaded library exports not yet applied,
  oldest first, then reads the RSS feed (#204). The newer of the two wins, field by field, so
  an old export can't undo what RSS already pushed, and the first export after the RSS
  backfill pushes nothing. A dated export can be the backfill. Stored exports also tell the
  RSS feed which of the profile's shelves are tags.
