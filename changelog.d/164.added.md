- The Goodreads sync's failure policy (#164). A transient Hardcover failure (5xx, timeout,
  network) is retried twice within the run, after 2 s and 8 s; a refusal (a 200 with an
  error, another 4xx) is not retried; `403 request_exceeds_capacity` is a bug and is
  quarantined at once. A change still failing stays pending, counting its attempts, and is
  quarantined once it has failed for 24 hours and 3 times. It's released by a new Goodreads
  value, or once by each new SyncShelf version at startup (logged as `quarantine_released`).
  The daily Hardcover reserve defers the run, counting no failure. Stored errors are
  scrubbed.
