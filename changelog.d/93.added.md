- `remove_book` takes a book off the shelves (#93): its status, rating, review, reads and the
  reading-journal history Hardcover would otherwise leave behind. `correct_read(delete=true)`
  deletes one read. Both preview first and act only on the confirm token that preview returns,
  good for 10 minutes and for that one book or read; if the book changed since, they preview
  again and delete nothing. A removal returns every value the book had, for re-adding it.
