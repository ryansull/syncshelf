- The Goodreads matcher chain (#163): the Goodreads mapping, then ISBN-13, then ISBN-10, then
  a fuzzy title-and-author search, the first step with a hit deciding. An id hit whose title
  scores under 60 is queued `id_title_mismatch`; an id naming several books is queued
  `ambiguous_mapping`; a clear fuzzy match links outside the backfill, and anything else is
  queued `fuzzy_low`, `fuzzy_ambiguous` or `no_hit` with up to 5 candidates. A queued book is
  re-matched when its details change. A link is replaced only by a strictly stronger method,
  and never a human one.
